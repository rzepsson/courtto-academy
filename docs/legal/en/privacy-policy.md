## 2. The essential point: we process data in two distinct roles

This distinction determines who to address requests about data to.

### First role — we are the controller

This concerns **your account data**: name, email address, password, sign-in history, notifications and subscription billing data. We determine the purposes and means of processing this data. **This policy describes that role.**

### Second role — we are a processor

This concerns **the data a school enters into the application about its students and their guardians**: participant profiles, guardian contact details, consents, class enrolments, attendance and class fee settlements.

The controller of that data is **the school**, not us. We process it solely on the school's documented instructions under a data processing agreement. We do not use it for our own purposes, do not analyse it and do not disclose it to anyone beyond the parties listed in section 5.

> **If you are a student or the parent of a student and want to know what data is processed about you, to correct it or to have it deleted — contact your school.** We have no right to dispose of that data independently, and the school as controller has context we do not. If we receive such a request directly, we will pass it to the school and inform you that we have done so.

Note that one person may occupy both roles. A coach has an account with us (first role) and is simultaneously a member of a school (second role). A request to delete an account does not delete the records the school keeps about that person as an employee or as a class participant.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

1. **access** your data and obtain a copy (art. 15);
2. **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data (art. 16);
3. **erasure** (art. 17) — save for data whose retention is required by law, such as accounting records;
4. **restriction** of processing (art. 18);
5. **portability** of data processed on the basis of a contract or consent, in a structured, machine-readable format (art. 20);
6. **object** to processing based on legitimate interests (art. 21);
7. **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal (art. 7(3)).

**Several of these rights can be exercised directly in the application**, under *Account*: changing your details, changing your email address, downloading a copy of your data, signing out other devices, withdrawing marketing consent, and deleting your account.

Other requests should be sent to **[privacy@courtto.pl]**. We respond without undue delay and within one month at the latest; in complex matters this may be extended by two months, of which we will inform you.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the **President of the Personal Data Protection Office** (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland.

Providing data is voluntary but necessary to conclude and perform the agreement — without it an account cannot be created. Providing data for marketing purposes is entirely voluntary and does not affect your ability to use the application.

## 8. Cookies and similar technologies

**We do not track you and do not display a consent banner, because there is nothing to ask consent for.**

The application uses only files necessary for it to function:

| File | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Better Auth session cookie | Keeping you signed in. Authentication does not work without it. | Strictly necessary | Until sign-out or session expiry |
| Signed session copy (`cookieCache`) | Reducing database queries on each request | Strictly necessary | 5 minutes |
| `courtto_locale` | Remembering your chosen interface language | Functional | 12 months |

Under art. 398(3) of the Electronic Communications Law, storing information necessary to provide a service requested by the user does not require consent.

We do not use analytics tools, advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, embedded maps, embedded video, or fonts loaded from external servers — fonts are self-hosted.

You may block cookies in your browser settings, but this will make signing in impossible.

## 9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, in particular:

- transport encryption using TLS;
- storage of passwords solely as cryptographic hashes — we do not know your password and cannot read it;
- logical isolation of each school's data, enforced at the database query level and covered by automated tests;
- role-based access control;
- logging of member and financial operations in a log that cannot be edited;
- error and incident monitoring;
- backups;
- data minimisation by design — we do not collect data we do not need.

In the event of a personal data breach resulting in a high risk to your rights, we will notify you without undue delay and the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours.

## 10. Data of persons under 18

The application is intended for entrepreneurs. A minor may hold an account only with the consent of their legal representative; verifying that consent rests with the school inviting the participant.

Data of minor class participants is processed by us **solely as a processor, on a school's instructions** (the second role in section 2). The legal basis, scope and information obligation towards the child and their guardian are determined by the school as controller.
